02 · HOW I ACTUALLY WORK

My rules, learned the hard way.

Nobody taught me these in a textbook. The help desk trenches, Tier 2 escalations, and strict governance audits did.

  1. Answer like a human, then troubleshoot like a machine.First thirty seconds: who is stuck, what died, how many trucks are queuing? Then logs, then diagnostics. Panic drops when someone actually listens — systems recover when you follow the sequence.
  2. No ticket, no existence (The Iron Law of Governance).Every single check, alert, change, and build must have a corresponding ticket in Odoo with evidence. If you tweak a production server, deploy an Action1 update, or touch SQL backups without an Odoo ticket and a rollback plan, that’s not IT support — that’s gambling. Daily ops are only complete when ticket hygiene in Odoo is 100%.
  3. Automate the repetitive misery, but own the script.My PowerShell onboarding scripts and Excel dashboards save hours every week. But if my script hiccups, that’s my ticket to fix. Automation is leverage, not an excuse to dodge responsibility.
  4. Fix the cause, not just the symptom (The Tier 2 Oath).On Tier 1, a quick reboot gets the queue moving. On Tier 2, if you reboot without reading event logs, checking environmental factors (power/temp), and finding the root cause, you just scheduled your next outage for tomorrow. I patch configs, tune alert thresholds with senior engineering, and escalate clean to Dev & IT Admin.
